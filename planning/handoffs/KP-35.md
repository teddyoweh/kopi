# KP-35 — Bid workspace UI and Bids list

**Built**
- **`/bid?doc=…`** (`components/bid/bid-view.tsx`), the bid workspace:
  - **Header**: agency disc, title (`displayTitle`), a live countdown (red under 48 hours),
    method, and "Bidding as <company>".
  - **Stage stepper** (`stage-stepper.tsx`): Qualify, Clarify, Draft, Review, Submit from
    `memory.stage`, with "Next: <next_step>" under it. On phones it shows bars plus "Draft ·
    stage 3 of 5".
  - **Activity**: the bid's conversation, rendered with the copilot's `AssistantTurn` and
    `UserMessage`. The kickoff request shows as "You started the bid, <time>. Kopi works it
    from here."
    - Before the bid starts, a card explains what Kopi will do, with **Start the bid**.
    - The composer is the copilot's, exported, with the tender chip fixed.
  - **Rail**:
    - **Documents** (`documents-card.tsx`): drafts in reading order (bid plan,
      clarification questions, compliance matrix, checklist, proposal outline), each opening
      the preview sheet or downloading. Uploads can be added: `.pdf .md .txt .csv`, up to
      8 MB, checked in the browser first.
    - **Bid memory** (`memory-card.tsx`): Kopi's and the person's notes with their age, a
      note field, and forget on hover.
    - **Submission checklist** (`checklist-card.tsx`): ticks and a progress bar.
  - **Behaviour:**
    - The conversation is kept per tender in localStorage (`kopi.bidTurns.<doc>`); the
      memory and documents live on the API.
    - `&start=1` sends the kickoff once and drops the parameter, so a reload doesn't start
      the bid again.
    - The first event with a `session_id` attaches it to the bid (`attachSession`).
    - Memory refreshes after `remember` and `set_bid_stage` results; files refresh on
      `file` events. Both refresh at the end of each turn.
- **`/bids`** (`bids-view.tsx`) replaces Submissions. It lists open and closed bids by
  deadline, each with its stage, next step, document count and countdown. A bid that
  hasn't started shows **Start**. `/submissions` redirects to `/bids/`.
- **Sidebar:** "Bids" (briefcase) replaces "Submissions", with a bid count. It is active on
  `/bids`, `/bid` and `/submissions`.
- **Tender page:** `BidButton` (Start bid, or Open bid) replaces Track this tender.
- **Mock** (`lib/mock-copilot.ts`): "Start the bid for <doc>…" in a bid session runs a
  scripted kickoff:
  - it reads the company, the notice, eligibility and the market;
  - it remembers three facts (closing and format, what to settle, the price band) through
    `Beat.effect` writing to `lib/mock-memory`;
  - it moves qualify → clarify → draft;
  - it writes five documents (`bidPlanDraft` with a timeline back from closing,
    clarification questions, compliance matrix, checklist, `proposalOutlineDraft`);
  - it ends with its call and what it needs from the person.
- The copilot's step list describes `remember` ("Remembered: …") and `set_bid_stage`
  ("Moved the bid to …"), with Brain and Flag icons.

**Changed outside my paths (small, additive):**
- `lib/api.ts` and `lib/mock.ts`: `sessionBlob()`, so uploaded PDFs download as bytes
  rather than text.
- `components/draft-preview.tsx`: `downloadDraft` uses it for anything that isn't `.md`.
- `components/tender-view.tsx`: uses `BidButton`.
- `components/submissions-view.tsx` was removed; its pieces moved into
  `components/bid/*`.

**Where the agent went wrong**
- My first phone layout put the rail (documents, memory, checklist) above the activity,
  and the five stage labels truncated to "Qua…". The activity now comes first, and phones
  get bars plus one line naming the stage.
- Downloads went through `sessionFile().text()`, which would have corrupted uploaded PDFs.
  I added `sessionBlob` before any upload could be downloaded.
