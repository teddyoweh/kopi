# KP-33 — Rich search result cards with preview pane

**Built** (`web/components/search/*`, `search-view.tsx`)
- **Cards** (`result-card.tsx`):
  - header: an agency initials disc, then agency · category, then the actions (Start bid,
    Ask Kopi, Open);
  - title in `displayTitle`, with the API's highlights, linking to the tender;
  - a "why it matched" snippet (`insight.snippet`) with the query's words marked;
  - chips: a match meter (four bars, Loose to Strong, exact score in the tooltip),
    eligibility, closing, a price band from past awards, method, and two envelopes.
  - Start bid is filled only on the selected card and outlined on the rest, so a page of
    results isn't a wall of indigo.
  - On phones the actions move to the card's foot, and the method and envelope chips are
    hidden.
- **Eligibility chip** (`chips.tsx`), from `EligibilitySummary`:
  - the closing check is always one check, so nothing else checked reads "No registration
    named";
  - a blocker reads "Blocker · EPU/CMP/10 at S6" (the code, via `shortRequirement`);
  - one unknown reads "Confirm …", several read "N to confirm";
  - otherwise "Eligible · N checks met".
  - Full reasons are in the tooltips.
- **Insights** (`use-insights.ts`):
  - fetched after the hits render, 25 per call, for the cards shown ("Show all" fetches the
    rest);
  - cached per profile and query, so going back to a search doesn't refetch;
  - a failed batch drops its cards' skeletons and keeps the cards.
- **Preview pane** (`preview-pane.tsx`, at 1280px and wider): the selected tender's facts,
  eligibility list, what similar work sold for (median, middle half, count, most frequent
  winner), items and a description excerpt, with the same actions. It is sticky in the
  panel, and details are cached per profile.
- **Keyboard** (`results.tsx`): j/k or the arrow keys move the selection (scrolled into
  view), Enter opens it, and b starts its bid. Keys typed in fields, and Enter on links and
  buttons, are left alone. Below 1280px a card click opens the tender.
- **Start bid** (`actions.tsx`, `useStartBid`) uses `lib/bids.startBid` and goes to
  `/bid/?doc=…&start=1` (it says "Open bid" once a bid exists). The `/bid` page is KP-35's.
