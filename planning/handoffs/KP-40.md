# KP-40: Sidebar as the workspace (Inbox, your bids, saved Views)

**Done**
- **Sidebar** (`web/components/shell/nav.tsx`, `app-shell.tsx`):
  - The top items are Inbox (unread count), Home, Copilot and Bids.
  - **Your bids** lists open bids, soonest first. Each shows a stage ring (`StageRing` in
    `bid/stage-stepper.tsx`), a short title (`lib/short-title.ts`) and the days left, red
    inside two days.
  - **Views** lists saved searches with the count of notices published since each was last
    opened. A "+" leads to Search.
  - The search icon opens the ⌘K palette, with a tooltip showing the keys. A `?` button sits
    at the bottom, and phones get a palette button in the top bar.
  - The sidebar is 16.5rem wide so "Pragnition Labs" fits beside the icons. Long names now
    truncate instead of running under them.
- **Saved views** (`lib/views.ts`, `components/search/save-view.tsx`):
  - Save view names the search in a popover.
  - A saved search shows its name as the page title (Views / name).
  - Opening a view marks it seen, and changing its words or filters leaves it.
  - Two views are seeded (Closing this week, IT & telecoms), so the section shows what it
    is for.
  - `runSearch` and `readSearchQuery` moved here from the Search page, so views, the Inbox
    and Search run one search.
- **Shared bid status** (`lib/bid-status.ts`):
  - One store fetches each session's memory and files at most once a minute for the
    sidebar, Home, Bids and Inbox.
  - The bid page primes it, so a stage Kopi moves shows everywhere at once.
- **Toasts:**
  - "Now bidding as …" when the company changes;
  - Saved and Removed a view, with Undo;
  - Stopped bidding, with Undo (`useBids().restoreBid`).

**Decisions:** D30.
- The sidebar reads `?doc=` and `?view=`, so the nav renders inside `<Suspense>`. The static
  export needs that, and the shell is client-only behind the access gate, so nothing flashes.
- A view's matches are the hits within 0.12 of its best, never below 0.30 (`matchesOf` in
  `lib/views.ts`), or every semantic view would count its whole 50.

**For the next agent**
- `useBidStatus(session)` and `useBidStatuses(sessions)` come from `lib/bid-status.ts`; don't
  fetch `api.memory` for display anywhere else.
- `useMatches(queries)` is the shared, five-minute cache of what a search finds.

**Where the agent went wrong**
- The first Inbox and view counts on live data used every hit a view's search returned. The
  "AI and chatbots" view counted 25 new notices, mostly manpower tenders. Mock data (30
  notices) could not show it. It was caught on the live screenshots and fixed with the
  relative floor, measured on three real queries.
- `/tmp` was wiped between sessions, taking the QA scripts with it. The rig now lives in
  `data/qa/` (gitignored), with `shoot40.mjs` for these pages.
