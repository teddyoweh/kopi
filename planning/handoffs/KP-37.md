# KP-37 — Bid workspace as a chat with an artifacts panel

**Why:** Teddy, 29 Sep: working a bid "should feel like ChatGPT / artifacts panel, same page,
clean". The KP-35 layout put documents in a side rail and opened them in a sheet over the
page.

**Built**
- **`/bid` goes full-bleed** (`components/shell/app-shell.tsx`, `BLEED`). The page sets its own
  height (`100dvh` minus the 53px top bar, and minus the 16px inset from lg), and each pane
  scrolls on its own.
- **Left pane, the chat** (`bid-view.tsx`, 42% wide, 26–40rem):
  - a compact header: agency, countdown, a two-line title, and a `StageLine` (five segments,
    "Draft · proposal, matrix, checklist · 3 of 5", with the next step under it);
  - the turns, which follow the stream unless the reader has scrolled up;
  - the composer pinned to the foot.
- **Right pane, the artifacts** (`artifact-panel.tsx`):
  - **Tabs:** pill tabs in reading order with short names (Plan, Questions, Matrix,
    Checklist, Outline; the full name is in the tooltip), then uploads (paperclip), then
    Memory N and Tasks done/total. The open tab scrolls into view.
  - **Documents:** render as markdown in a 46rem column, with Copy and Download.
  - **Live writing:** a `Write` call's content shows at once with a "Kopi is writing this"
    badge; an `Edit` clears the live copy, and the saved file is refetched on its `file`
    event (versions bump).
  - **Following:** while a turn runs, the panel moves to whatever Kopi is writing, unless
    the person picked a tab during that turn.
  - **Uploads:** PDFs render in place in an iframe from a blob URL, and text files as
    text. **Add tender documents** sits in the panel header.
  - **Memory and Tasks** are panes (`memory-pane.tsx` and `checklist-pane.tsx`, the old
    cards without their chrome). The tickable list is called **Tasks**, so it doesn't clash
    with Kopi's own `checklist.md`.
- **File cards in the chat** open the document in the panel. On phones they also switch
  to the Documents view.
- **Phones:** a Chat / Documents N toggle under the top bar.
- Removed `documents-card.tsx`, the sheet preview on the bid page, and the full-width
  `StageStepper` (`STAGES` and `stageLabel` stay for `/bids`).

**Checked**
- Lint and the mock build are clean.
- The mock kickoff at 1440 and 390 (`/tmp/kopi-shots/shoot-bid3.mjs`):
  - the panel shows each document as it is written, and ends on the Outline;
  - tabs read Plan | Questions | Matrix | Checklist | Outline | Memory 3 | Tasks 0/8;
  - adding a note puts it at the top of Memory;
  - on phones, Documents 5 shows the open tab in view;
  - no console errors or overflow.
- Shots: `artifacts/media/kopi/art-*`.

**Where the agent went wrong**
- The first panel had two tabs named "Checklist": Kopi's `checklist.md` and the rule-built
  tick list. The QA script's locator hit both, which is how I noticed. The tick list is
  now "Tasks".
- With full document names and an icon on every tab, the tabs overflowed at 1440 and the
  open tab (Outline) scrolled out of sight. The fix: short names, icons only where they
  tell tabs apart, the upload label shown only at 2xl, and the open tab scrolled into
  view, including again when a phone switches to Documents.
