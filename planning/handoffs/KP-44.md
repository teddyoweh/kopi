# KP-44: Autopilot on the bid page

**Done**
- **Start bid starts the autopilot.** `&start=1` sets `bid.autopilot = "on"` and sends the
  autopilot request.
- **The chain.** After each step the page reads the bid memory and sends "Continue on
  autopilot". It stops at:
  - submit: done, the Pack tab opens, and a toast says "Ready to submit";
  - a "No bid:" next step: done;
  - a step that didn't move the stage, or six turns: paused, with a toast;
  - a pause from the person: the current step finishes first;
  - an error: paused.
- **Leaving the page** ends the stream but keeps the autopilot on, and reopening the bid
  resumes it.
- **`AutopilotBar`** (`components/bid/autopilot-bar.tsx`) shows one of:
  - "Run on autopilot" for a bid started by hand (such as Teddy's MHASPF03000029475);
  - step n of 4, with what it is writing, and Pause;
  - Paused, with Resume;
  - Ready to submit, with Open the pack and Download all (every document as one markdown
    file);
  - Kopi's call: no bid.
- **The chat and the panel.**
  - Autopilot requests show as quiet lines ("Autopilot took the next step"), not as
    messages from the person.
  - The panel has Pack, Proposal, Cover letter, Pricing and Risks tabs, with the pack first.
- **Bids list:** an Autopilot pill, and "Ready to submit" at the submit stage.
- **Demo mode:** `lib/mock-autopilot.ts` plays the same four steps. Its drafts state
  assumptions instead of placeholders. It runs end to end in about 25 s.
- **QA:** `data/qa/shoot-autopilot.mjs`, mock at 1440 and 390, no console errors.

**Decisions:** D31. The page drives the steps because one turn can't hold a whole bid (600 s
API limit), and steps make progress visible and pausable.

**Where the agent went wrong**
- The first QA wait matched "Ready to submit." before the bar showed it, so the "done"
  screenshot caught step 4 mid-write. It now waits for the status bar itself.
- On a phone, the Ready bar squeezed its text into a one-word column instead of wrapping the
  buttons below. The text now keeps a 12rem minimum.
- The stage line lowercased its hint, which turned "GeBIZ" into "gebiz". Only the first letter
  is lowered now.
