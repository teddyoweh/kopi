# KP-41: Home and Inbox built around the person's bids

**Done**
- **Home** (`web/components/home/`, and `app/page.tsx`; it was Overview), top to bottom:
  - the date and a greeting, with links to the Inbox and your bids and a count of what
    GeBIZ published today;
  - **Get started**: four steps, each ticked from real state (profile edited, a tender
    opened, a bid started, a view saved), dismissible;
  - **Your bids**: stage ring, stage and next step, countdown;
  - **Next two weeks**: fourteen columns of GeBIZ closings per Singapore day, with your
    bid deadlines in indigo, each day linking to Search's closing window;
  - the stats, best matches and newest from `overview.tsx`, which now only exports those
    sections.
- **Inbox** (`/inbox`; `lib/inbox.ts`, `components/inbox/inbox-view.tsx`):
  - **Items**:
    - one per bid: its deadline if inside 7 days (a new item again inside 48 hours),
      otherwise its next step, or "not started";
    - new notices from the past week near the top of the company's search or a view's.
  - **Read and done**: ids live in localStorage, and a changed next step is a new item.
  - **Layout**: the list sits beside a preview pane (the search `PreviewPane` for tenders,
    a bid summary for bids).
  - **Keys and actions**: j/k/↵/e, Mark all read, and Undo on done.
- "Closing in 7 days" means today plus seven Singapore days, and not already closed, in both
  the stat and the strip, as in Search's filter.
- **QA**: mock and live at 1440 and 390, with no console errors. Shots are
  `artifacts/media/kopi/platform-*`.

**Decisions:** D30. The Inbox is derived, not stored. There is no service to run and nothing
can drift from the bids, and read state is the only thing it keeps.

**For the next agent**
- `useInbox()` is also what the sidebar count and Home's greeting read. Keep it cheap: it
  reuses the shared bid status and search caches.
- The greeting uses the reader's own clock ("Good evening"); every date shown uses Singapore
  time.

**Where the agent went wrong**
- Home's stat and the new strip first disagreed (13 against 8) on the same page. Both
  counted "within 7 days" differently, and the stat also counted notices already past their
  deadline. They now share one definition.
- The stats read `Date.now()` during render, which the React lint rule rejects. They now use
  the ticking `useNow`.
