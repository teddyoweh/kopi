# KP-18 — Natural voiceover, cut and render

**Built**
- **`artifacts/media/kopi-demo.mp4`** (in the session): the master.
  - 3:58.6, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, H.264 (CRF 15) with 192 kbps AAC.
  - Voice normalised to −16.2 LUFS; 53 MB.
- **A web copy** at **https://kopi.unv.run/demo/kopi-demo.mp4**.
  - CRF 23, 24.4 MB, luma SSIM 0.9998 against the master.
  - It sits in `artifacts/apps/kopi/demo/`, outside `web/`, so it isn't in the repo. Copy
    it back after rebuilding the site.
  - The README links to it.
- **The voice** follows D23:
  - ElevenLabs `eleven_multilingual_v2`, premade voice "Will", at 1.07×.
  - Thirteen lines, one per shot, each generated with its neighbours as context.
  - Made through the signed-in web app on the default browser profile (free tier; 3,639
    of 10,000 characters used this month). No API key was created.
- **The cut** (`data/film-rig/cut.py`) follows the table in `planning/06-demo-script.md`:
  - Each line gets a slot: a 0.25 s lead-in, the line, and a 0.25 s gap.
  - Its footage is a list of source pieces at fixed speeds, plus one flexible piece that
    fills the slot, never faster than 2.5×.
  - Anything at 1.75× or faster carries a label with its exact speed ("8× speed" over the
    copilot's thinking, "2.1× speed" over the overview), so no wait is hidden.
  - A chapter label ("1 / 6 What it does" … "6 / 6 The weakest part, and what's next")
    opens each of the brief's six points.
  - Title cards open and close the film. They're set in the app's own font (Geist) and
    brand colour, flat, rendered from HTML in Chrome.

**Checked, not assumed**
- **Every section boundary was watched.** A frame at each segment start confirmed the
  right footage, chapter label and speed label. The first pass exposed two problems, both
  fixed: the draft preview had been cut from the wrong source range, and a flexible piece
  was running at 18.8×.
- **Sync.** Each segment's audio is cut to its video's exact sample count. Whisper
  timestamps on the final audio put every line within 0.3 s of its footage. The one
  outlier was whisper itself: an RMS trace shows s06 starting at exactly 105.0 s, after the
  intended silence over the copilot's answer.
- **Words.** All 13 lines were transcribed back and compared with the script.
- **Length.** 3:58.6, inside the 3–4 minute brief.

**Where the agent went wrong**
- **Assumed where the draft preview was.** The first cut took the preview from 73–82 s of
  the copilot clip, where the answer was still showing. The preview opens at 82.5 s in this
  take. Found by watching the boundary frames.
- **Let a flexible piece absorb anything.** When the fixed pieces nearly filled a slot, the
  flexible one was squeezed to 18.8×. It's now capped at 2.5×, and the slot runs long
  instead.
- **Audio drifted 0.23 s per segment.** `adelay` plus `atrim=duration` came out one
  lead-in short every time. Over 13 segments that would have put the last line nearly 3 s
  early. Each segment's audio is now cut to its video's sample count.
- **Rounded the speed labels at first.** A 2.25× piece was about to be labelled "2×".
  Labels are now rendered from the exact speed.
